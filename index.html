About Polymer Prodigy Labs

In industrial manufacturing, scaling up a chemical powder feed line traditionally requires massive upfront capital, fixed legacy hardware, and complex integration. We are changing that.

Our company has engineered a modular, additive feeder system that combines high-strength, advanced 3D-printed composite hardware with an intuitive, unified program. Run from a single touchscreen master unit, our feeders network together seamlessly via Ethernet, allowing plants to connect anywhere from one to 100 satellite units dynamically as production demands grow or your batching recipe requires.

Who We Partner With

We work directly with facility plant managers, automation procurement groups, and operations directors looking to optimize floor layout and aggressively minimize infrastructure capital expenditure by removing dependencies on vendor-locked custom metal fabrication loops.
